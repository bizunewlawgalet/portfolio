Place your profile photo here!
Recommended name: profile.jpg (or profile.png)
Dimensions: 400x400 pixels or square ratio.

To use your photo in the website:
1. Copy your picture into this assets/ folder and name it 'profile.jpg'.
2. In index.html, find the profile-avatar-container (around line 160) and change:
   <div class="avatar-fallback" id="profile-avatar-box">...</div>
   to:
   <img src="assets/profile.jpg" alt="Bizunew Lawgalet" class="profile-img">
